Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who we are

Tortopani is the online pastry school of Liliia Poliakova, operating as a registered sole proprietor (FOP) in Ukraine. We are responsible for the personal data described below.

Contact us about privacy via Telegram or Instagram.

What data we collect and why

  • When you buy. Payment is handled by WayForPay, a licensed payment provider. We receive the details you enter at checkout, such as your name, email, phone number and order details, so we can deliver your e-book and support you. We never see or store your card details. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • When you message us. If you write to us on Telegram or Instagram, we see what you send and use it only to answer you.
  • Advertising measurement. Only if you accept cookies (see below).

We do not sell your personal data.

We keep order data for as long as we need it to give you access to your purchase and to meet accounting and tax obligations. Messages are kept for as long as the conversation is useful to you.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, or give it to you in a portable format. You can withdraw cookie consent at any time with the button above. If you live in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
